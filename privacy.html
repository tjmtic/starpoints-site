Privacy Policy

Starpoints for Android (com.abyxcz.starpoints) · Effective [date it goes live]

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • Starpoints has no internet permission, so the app cannot connect to the internet. It has no servers.
  • The camera and your location are used on your phone only, while AR mode is open. Camera images are never saved, and your location is never saved.
  • What the app does save (your saved stars and views, settings, quiz score and Guide voice) stays in the app's private storage on your phone, and in your own Android backup if you use one.
  • The on-device language model is delivered by Google Play after install, the same way the app is.

1. What Starpoints does

Starpoints draws the night sky from a star catalog stored inside the app: stars to magnitude 8, the 88 constellations, and the 110 Messier objects. Map mode is a chart you drag and pinch, and it needs no permissions. AR mode draws the same sky over your camera's live view, placed using your location, the time and the way you hold the phone. The Ask box, the Quiz reactions and the Guide use a small language model that runs on the phone. All of this is computed on your device.

4. The on-device model and Google Play

Ask, the Quiz reactions and the Guide use MiniCPM5-2B (by OpenBMB, Apache License 2.0), run by llama.cpp on your phone. The model is about 1.56 GB. Google Play downloads it as an asset pack straight after the app installs, and may wait for Wi-Fi before it does. Until it arrives, everything else in the app works.

To deliver the right pack, Google's Play Asset Delivery library tells Google Play your device's metadata and the app's version. Google describes this data as encrypted, not transferred to third parties, and deleted after a fixed retention period. That exchange is between your phone and Google Play and is covered by Google's privacy policy. Starpoints never receives it.

Everything you type into Ask and everything the model writes stays on the phone. Facts about a star (its brightness, colour, position) are computed from the catalog, never by the model. Guide entries are labelled as fiction.

5. Backups

Android backs up apps' files to your Google account when backup is switched on for your phone. Starpoints' saved points, settings, quiz score and Guide voice may be included. That backup belongs to your Google account and is governed by Google's privacy policy; Starpoints cannot read it. You can exclude the app or turn backups off in your phone's settings.

6. Third parties and credits

Starpoints contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and shares nothing with third parties. The only Google component in the app is Play Asset Delivery (section 4). Starpoints is distributed through Google Play, which has its own privacy policy covering downloads and updates.

The star catalog comes from d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn (BSD 3-Clause License), and its Messier data from SEDS. Both are credited, with the model's licence, on the app's About screen.

7. Deleting your data

Everything Starpoints keeps is on your phone, so you delete it yourself: delete saved points in the app, clear the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall the app, which removes all of it. There is nothing to request from us, because we hold nothing.

8. Children

Starpoints is a general-audience astronomy app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.
